About All United FC

Founded in 2022, All United FC began in the All Nations League with a simple purpose: to give everyone a chance to play soccer, meet new friends, and feel part of a team.

What started as a shared love of the game has grown into a community. Through every match, we’ve built friendships, supported one another, and developed together as players and teammates.

Today, we’re preparing to compete in another league and take on new challenges. As we grow, our purpose stays the same: bringing people together through soccer and making everyone feel welcome.

One club. One mission. All United.
